What CRIP Does

Everything you need to stop losing customers

Built for small and mid-size businesses that can't afford Gainsight or ChurnZero — but still need to know who's about to leave.

Upload Any CSV — Instant Analysis

No API keys. No integrations. Export your customer data from any CRM, ERP, or spreadsheet and upload it. CRIP auto-detects your columns and runs the ML engine immediately.

Risk Scores With Real Explanations

Every customer gets a churn probability score and a SHAP-based reason in plain language. Not "anomaly detected" — but "Month-to-month contract and 2-month tenure are driving this customer's 88% risk score."

Customer Lifetime Value Prediction

See projected CLV per customer alongside risk score. Know not just who is leaving — know how much revenue is actually walking out the door, so you can prioritize retention spend.

Health Score & Cohort Analysis

A composite health score (0–100) per customer combining tenure, payment stability, and churn risk. Cohort charts show you which customer age group is churning fastest.

Proactive Alerts & Weekly Digest

Get an email the moment any customer crosses your risk threshold. Every Monday, receive a full weekly digest with revenue-at-risk, health averages, and the top 3 accounts to call this week.

AI Campaign Generator

Select your at-risk customers and let CRIP generate personalized retention emails powered by Gemini AI. A/B test variants automatically and track which message actually retained the customer.

How It Works

From spreadsheet to retention strategy in 4 steps

01

Upload Your Data

Export customers from any tool as a CSV. Paste a Google Sheets link. Or add records manually. CRIP accepts any structure — no column mapping required.

02

AI Runs in Seconds

Our hybrid ML engine (Random Forest + Isolation Forest) scores every customer for churn probability. SHAP values explain the top reasons per customer in plain English.

03

See Who's At Risk

Your dashboard instantly shows risk distribution, revenue at stake, health scores, and contract renewal calendar — sorted by urgency so you know where to look first.

04

Act Fast

Generate a personalized retention email, run a What-If simulation to test a discount offer, or set an automated alert so your team never misses a high-risk account.
