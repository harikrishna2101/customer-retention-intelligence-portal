Terms of Use

CRIP is a student project and prototype customer-retention intelligence portal. Do not upload real sensitive customer data unless the deployment has been reviewed, secured, and approved for that use.

Predictions, risk scores, and generated messages are decision-support outputs only. They should be reviewed by a human before business action is taken.

By using this prototype, you agree to use it responsibly, keep credentials private, and avoid uploading data that you do not have permission to process.
